# Fun Industrial + Elektron: EV conversion evidence gate V1

**Business mission:** support safe, repeatable commercial gasoline/diesel-to-electric vehicle conversions. This module creates an evidence readiness dossier for an initial 2021 Ford F-450 source collection, not a conversion design or approval.

It reads V12R's source-integrity report, validates its hash against V12P, and enumerates missing verified inputs for seven domains: exact vehicle identity and axle ratings, duty cycle, battery integration, propulsion, safety, regulatory applicability, and manufacturing/validation.

All fields remain MISSING_VERIFIED_EVIDENCE until independently qualified evidence is supplied through a separately reviewed process. Readable extracted text is *not* engineering authority. No battery sizing, CAD, electrical design, procurement, physical work, HVIP eligibility, safety certification or production release is claimed.

The next engineering milestone requires choosing one exact VIN/configuration and collecting authoritative OEM body-builder/service information, qualified component specifications, duty-cycle measurements and regulatory review. Fun Industrial provides analysis and optimization; Elektron engineering verification must independently qualify requirements and test evidence. No service restarts or cross-project writes are performed.
