# EV conversion integration contracts V1

This milestone defines two integration groups and eight machine-readable capability contracts. CAD/wiring candidates: FreeCAD, KiCad, WireViz. Engineering/operations candidates: PostgreSQL, OpenModelica, Optuna, OR-Tools, ERPNext, pytest. Their presence in the manifest **does not mean installed or connected**.

The runner checks source-bound evidence and dependencies, then reports every adapter blocked until verified inputs and qualification. No mechanical drawings, schematics, simulation, procurement, safety certification, manufacturing work, or cross-project writes are executed. Elektron retains independent engineering approval and the operator must approve external effects. Resource-aware, sandboxed adapters and qualified engineering evidence are the next implementation stage.
